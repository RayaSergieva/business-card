## ✨ About

A single-page **interactive business card** that lives on the web. Click or tap it for a smooth **3D flip** to the back, where you'll find live contact links and a scannable QR code straight to my portfolio.

> 💡 Click the card to flip it. The links are live, and the QR works with any phone camera.

<br/>

## 🎨 Features

&nbsp;🔄&nbsp; **3D flip animation** on click or tap
&nbsp;📱&nbsp; **Fully responsive** across phone, tablet, and desktop
&nbsp;🎯&nbsp; **Scannable QR code** that opens the portfolio instantly
&nbsp;🖱️&nbsp; **Parallax tilt** that follows your cursor
&nbsp;⌨️&nbsp; **Keyboard accessible** (Tab, then Enter or Space)
&nbsp;🌈&nbsp; **Gradient design** with soft floating accent shapes

## 💡 How the Flip Works

The card rotates on the Y-axis with CSS 3D transforms. JavaScript drives the rotation through an *ease-in-out* curve and swaps the visible face right at the edge-on point (90°), so the card never looks transparent mid-flip. The back face starts pre-rotated 180° so its text reads correctly once flipped.
